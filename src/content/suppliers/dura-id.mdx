## Company at a glance

Dura-ID Solutions is a UK-based specialist manufacturer of chemical and industrial labels. It was formed on 1 February 2018 through the merger of three established label businesses — IML Labels & Systems, Longcombe Labels, and Leedmarque Concepts — into a combined company operating from multiple UK production sites. That heritage matters: while the Dura-ID name is relatively young, the underlying manufacturing experience is not.

In this directory, Dura-ID is the clear **EU/UK option**. Where Brady and Seton are global-through-US and DuraLabel, Avery, and OnlineLabels are US-anchored, Dura-ID's entire orientation is toward UK and European chemical-labelling law. It describes its remit explicitly in terms of GHS legislation "also referred to as CLP legislation in the EU" — which is exactly the framing a European buyer needs.

## GHS and CLP positioning

Dura-ID's specialism is chemical labelling for GHS and CLP compliance, and this is its strongest differentiator against the US-focused suppliers. Rather than treating CLP as an afterthought, it builds for it: bespoke chemical labels, multi-language capability, and an emphasis on guiding buyers through compliance rather than simply shipping stock.

A particular strength for European operations is multi-language labelling. Because CLP sale across member states often requires labels in several languages, Dura-ID's ability to combine GHS, CLP, ADR transport information, and variable batch data on a single label — with print resolution that supports additional languages and statements — directly addresses a pain point that the US-anchored suppliers in this directory do not.

## BS5609 and full 3-part compliance

Dura-ID is explicit about full marine compliance. It states it can help a buyer achieve **full 3-part BS5609 compliance** — covering the adhesive, the face material, and the overprinting — by combining its BS5609 labels with a tested and approved print solution. This is the complete marine-grade picture (not just Section 2 base material), with the same honest caveat that applies across this category: Section 3 print durability depends on pairing the certified label stock with an approved printer, so the certification belongs to the label-and-printer system, not the label in isolation.

For drum and IBC shipping under IMDG and CLP, that full 3-part capability — paired with multi-language support — makes Dura-ID well suited to European marine shippers in particular.

## Where Dura-ID fits in a procurement decision

Dura-ID suits the buyer who wants a partner rather than a checkout. Its model is consultative: chemical-labelling specialists assess the application and recommend a solution spanning labels, printers, and compliance software, and it manufactures bespoke labels to custom sizes and shapes. That is a strong fit for an operation with specific or complex requirements — multi-language CLP labels, unusual container formats, or full marine compliance — that benefits from expert guidance.

The trade-off is the flip side of the same model. A buyer who wants to order standard GHS labels instantly online, at a published price, with no consultation, will find the self-serve US suppliers (Avery, OnlineLabels) more immediate. Dura-ID's quote-based, advisory approach is built for considered procurement, not impulse purchase.

## How to evaluate Dura-ID for your facility

1. **Are you in the EU or UK?** This is Dura-ID's home ground. For CLP compliance and multi-language labels, it is the most directly aligned supplier in this directory.
2. **Do you need multi-language labels?** If you sell across EU member states and need several languages (plus ADR and batch data) on one label, this is a core Dura-ID strength.
3. **Is full 3-part BS5609 required?** For marine shipment, confirm the specific label-and-printer combination that delivers full Section 2 + 3 compliance.
4. **Do you want consultation or self-serve?** Dura-ID is consultative and quote-based. If you prefer instant online ordering at a fixed price, weigh that against a self-serve supplier.
